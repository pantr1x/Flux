Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Flux is made for learning and building – from your first print() to a full website.

Live Server

See your website change as you type

Your page right next to the code, reloading every time you save.

  • ⌥Alt + click any element and Flux jumps to its line in the HTML.
  • !JavaScript errors show up on the page with a Show in Flux button.
  • ▭Phone, tablet or laptop frames – rotate them, take a screenshot.
  • ⌗Scan a QR code to open the page on your real phone.
Make it yours

Themes, colors, plugins

Pick a code theme, your accent and app colors, fonts, cursor and wallpaper – or install plugins with one click.

  • ◐Themes – or build your own in the theme studio.
  • ✦Plugins – Theme Pack, Glass UI, Snippets, Flux Together and more.
  • ⚡Save memory on slower PCs – and fine-tune every part yourself under Advanced.
  • 🌐9 app languages, downloaded only when you pick one.
